Blood DK on single-target bosses

Cast breakdown from the same 12 highest-DPS Season 2 Blood Death Knight logs, restricted to boss pulls with exactly one enemy type — and split by Heroism.

Source: Warcraft Logs zone 55 (Mythic+ Season 2), keys 18–19. Single-target bosses: Zul'jan (7), Rav'i (4), Nalorakk (4). 15 pulls, 3566 seconds. Rates are casts per minute of time in that state, so segments of different length compare fairly.

First, the thing that broke the question

None of these players ever used Heroism on a boss. All 23 lust applications across the 12 logs land on trash — typically the opening pull around 25 seconds in, and one mid-run pull. Zero landed inside a boss encounter.

So a "single-target boss under Heroism" breakdown has no data behind it in this sample. Rather than invent one, the comparison below gives you the single-target boss profile as its own column, and the real Heroism-vs-no-Heroism split measured where lust actually gets used: on trash.

Why single target changes everything

0.99Infliction of Sorrow procs per Vampiric Strike — single-target boss
3.79same ratio on trash

On a lone boss the ratio is essentially exactly 1.00. That is the San'layn proc behaving exactly as written: Vampiric Strike consumes Blood Plague once per target, so with one target you get one proc. On trash the same button returns roughly four.

This is why the trash playbook does not transfer. Death and Decay and Blood Boil were worth 10x on trash because they widened the disease spread. Against one target there is nothing to spread to, and Infliction of Sorrow falls from 33.5 to 9.5 casts per minute.

Heroism raises total button rate by about 29% (83.9 against 64.8 per minute) — that is the haste. But Vampiric Strike climbs far more than that, and Infliction of Sorrow more than doubles. Vampiric Strike is proc-driven off Death Strike, so faster casting compounds into more procs, and Essence of the Blood Queen's stacking haste feeds back into it.

Read the Heroism column with care. Lust is used on the biggest pulls, so those segments have both more haste and more targets. Part of that proc gain is pull size, not the buff.

What to do differently on a single-target boss

  1. Stop optimising for spread. Death and Decay and pre-pull Blood Boil were the big trash levers because they multiplied Infliction of Sorrow across targets. At one target that multiplier is gone.
  2. Keep Blood Boil on cooldown anyway — 10.4 per minute here — for Blood Plague uptime and Hemostasis, not for cleave.
  3. Vampiric Strike still matters, it just pays 1:1. Every Death Strike is a roll at it, which is why Death Strike stays at 15.4 per minute.
  4. Dancing Rune Weapon comes up 0.69 times a minute here. Chaining charges still extends the window, but without the per-target multiplier the payoff is much smaller than on trash.
  5. If your group will lust a boss, expect roughly the Heroism column's shape — more Death Strikes, more Vampiric Strikes — but with proc counts near the single-target ratio, not the trash one.

Caveats

Single-target here means WCL recorded exactly one enemy type in the pull. Bosses that summon adds mid-fight (The Writhing Coil, The Hoardmonger, Sentinel of Winter) are excluded, which is why only 15 of 36 boss pulls qualified.

All 12 logs are CN-region across two dungeons. The Heroism sample is 920 seconds — enough to compare rates, not enough to be authoritative about a rotation.

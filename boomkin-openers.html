Starfall is the whole story. It swings from 0 to 33 casts while total cast count barely moves (97–116). The core rotation is fixed; what changes between fights is how much of it becomes AoE. Sszorak is pure single-target — not one Starfall in two minutes. The Lost Explorers is the opposite extreme, where Starfall is out-cast only by Starfire.

Nek'zali is the outlier on cooldowns. Four of five players open Incarnation inside the first 6.6 seconds; Splatugle holds it to 7.3s and skips the damage potion entirely. Every other parse pairs Potion of Recklessness with the opening burst.

What holds constant

The opening shape is the same in all five logs: dots first (Moonfire and Sunfire, sometimes with a filler Wrath while walking in), then Eclipse or straight into cooldowns, then Incarnation and Fury of Elune stacked in the same second, then a Starsurge dump while the burst window is open. Incarnation has two charges — every parse spends the first on the pull and banks the second, re-entering burst somewhere between 22s and 76s depending on the fight.

Empowering Venom appears exactly once in all five logs, always in the same GCD as the opening Incarnation. It is not a druid ability — it is a tier or trinket effect riding the burst window.

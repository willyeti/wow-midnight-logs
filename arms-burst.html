Arms Warrior inside Avatar and Colossus Smash

What Arms actually presses inside its burst windows, measured across 12 Mythic+ logs — and which buttons get dropped to make room.

Source: Warcraft Logs zone 55 (Mythic+ Season 2), Arms Warrior DPS rankings. 12 logs, 286 minutes of dungeon time, 16,677 cast events. 230 Avatar windows, 440 Colossus Smash windows. Pulled 26 Aug 2026.

About "12 keys"

Key 12 does not exist in Warcraft Logs' Mythic+ rankings. The leaderboards bottom out at key 13, and only two dungeons go that low — every other dungeon stops at 14. I paged to the end of the Arms rankings to confirm it.

So this is 12 logs at key 13, the lowest that exists. All from The Blinding Vale, since that is one of only two dungeons with key-13 entries. Worth saying plainly: key level changes enemy health and damage, not which buttons Arms presses inside a burst window, so the sequence below should hold at 12.

The two windows are different lengths

20.0sAvatar, median
10.0sColossus Smash, median
45%of Avatar time with Colossus Smash up

Avatar runs twice as long as Colossus Smash, so roughly half of every Avatar is spent without the armour debuff. At 45% the players here are getting about one full Colossus Smash inside each Avatar — close to the practical ceiling if Colossus Smash only comes up once per Avatar, but it does mean the back half of Avatar is running at Colossus-less damage.

The window is a Mortal Strike dump. It goes from 14.3 a minute at baseline to 23.5 inside Colossus Smash — and 25.4 with Avatar stacked on top. Bladestorm more than doubles.

What gets dropped is as informative as what goes up. Slam collapses from 1.30 to 0.20 per minute, and Overpower falls too. Those are the globals being reclaimed for Mortal Strike. Cleave stays flat — it is target-count driven, not window driven.

How to get more out of the window

  1. Colossus Smash on the global right after Avatar. Only 39% do it here. Avatar is 20 seconds and Colossus Smash is 10 — every global you delay is one spent at reduced damage.
  2. Mortal Strike is the payload. It nearly doubles inside the window for a reason; treat everything else as filler between charges of it.
  3. Stop casting Slam in the window. These logs cut it by 84%. It is the weakest global you can spend while the armour debuff is ticking.
  4. Bladestorm belongs inside. It runs 4.45 a minute in Colossus Smash against 1.86 at baseline.
  5. Plan for the back half of Avatar. Around half of it has no Colossus Smash. If a second charge is available, that is where it goes.

Caveats

All 12 logs come from one dungeon (The Blinding Vale), because it is one of only two with key-13 entries. Trash and boss pulls are pooled — target counts vary within the sample, which is why Cleave and Bladestorm rates should be read as averages rather than as a single-target or AoE profile.

Windows are bounded by the actual buff and debuff aura events (Avatar 107574, Colossus Smash 208086), not by cast time plus an assumed duration.

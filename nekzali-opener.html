Fury of Elune is the earliest cooldown — median cast #5 at 3.8s, in every single parse. Incarnation follows at #8 around 4.9s, and the first Starsurge does not land until #11 at 6.2s. The opener is roughly five seconds of setup before any spending happens.

Starfall barely features — 2 of 14. Nek'zali opens as single-target.

Two things people do differently

First cast: Wrath 50%, Starfire 43%. Both are just a filler while closing to the boss — whichever your Eclipse state favours.

DoT order: Moonfire first in 64% of parses, Sunfire first in 36%. No meaningful consensus, and both are down inside the first two seconds either way.

The opener, as a plan

  1. Filler on the way in — Wrath or Starfire, whichever suits your Eclipse.
  2. Both DoTs immediately — Moonfire and Sunfire inside the first two seconds. Order does not matter.
  3. Eclipse if you want it — around cast 4, though only 57% bother.
  4. Fury of Elune around cast 5, near 3.8s. Every parse does this.
  5. Incarnation around cast 8, near 4.9s, with your potion and trinket in the same window.
  6. Then dump Starsurge from about cast 10 onward. Positions 12 and 13 are the most agreed-upon casts in the entire opener.

Caveats

14 parses is a small sample — enough to show the shape, not enough to call a 43%-versus-36% split meaningful. Treat anything under about 60% consensus as "no agreement".

Casts are ordered by log timestamp, so abilities sharing a global (a trinket fired alongside Incarnation, for example) can appear in either order. That is why Light's Potential, Empowering Venom and Potion of Recklessness drift across positions 7–10.

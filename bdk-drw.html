Blood DK: inside the Dancing Rune Weapon window

331 Dancing Rune Weapon windows on Mythic+ trash pulls, taken from the 12 highest-DPS Blood Death Knight logs in Season 2. What they press, and which three decisions actually move the damage.

Source: Warcraft Logs zone 55 (Mythic+ Season 2), Blood DK DPS rankings, keys 18–19. Windows bounded by the Dancing Rune Weapon buff (spell 81256) and filtered to pulls WCL marks as non-boss. Pulled 25 Aug 2026. 30621 cast events analysed.

The mechanic everything hangs on

4.20Infliction of Sorrow procs per Vampiric Strike
43%of all cast events in-window are that proc
8.0smedian window length

Infliction of Sorrow is not a button. Its median gap between events is 0.00s and 84% land within half a second of each other — far inside the GCD. It is the San'layn proc: Vampiric Strike striking a target with Blood Plague consumes the disease for damage, once per target. At 4.20 procs per Vampiric Strike, these logs are averaging roughly 4.20 diseased targets per strike.

So the window is not won by pressing more buttons. It is won by making sure that when Vampiric Strike lands, as many targets as possible carry Blood Plague.

The three levers, ranked by measured effect

Chaining the two Dancing Rune Weapon charges is the dominant lever and it is not close. Chained windows run a median 17.1s against 8.0s, but they also run at 1.74 procs/sec against 0.44 — four times the rate, not just four times the duration. Only 21% of windows in this sample were chained.

Death and Decay is close to mandatory. 94% of these windows had it down. The 6% that did not averaged 1.05 procs against 10.12.

Vampiric Blood works differently from the other two. Procs per Vampiric Strike barely move (4.26 with, 3.99 without) — what changes is the number of Vampiric Strikes, 2.81 against 1.45. It buys you more strikes, not better ones.

Blood Boil inside the 4s before the window is worth about +24% procs (10.60 against 8.55) — real but far behind chaining and Death and Decay.

How to run the window

  1. Get Death and Decay down first. Before the window, not during it. Skipping it costs about 90% of the window's damage.
  2. Blood Boil to spread Blood Plague across the pull in the few seconds before you press it. Every diseased target is another Infliction of Sorrow per Vampiric Strike.
  3. Press the second Dancing Rune Weapon charge inside the first. This is the single biggest lever in the data and only one window in five uses it.
  4. Overlap Vampiric Blood to generate more Vampiric Strikes.
  5. Then just play the priority. Death Strike and Heart Strike, taking Vampiric Strike on proc. The button order inside the window matters much less than the four setup decisions above.

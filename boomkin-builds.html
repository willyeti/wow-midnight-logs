Elune's Chosen is close to mandatory — 87.7% to 99.8% adoption across all six. But look at the DPS columns rather than the adoption columns: on five of six bosses Elune's Chosen leads by 6k–20k DPS.

the exception

Sszorak is the one fight where Keeper of the Grove actually wins — 155.2k against Elune's Chosen's 153.7k. It is also the fight with the lowest Elune's Chosen adoption of the six (87.7%, against 99.8% on The Twin Fangs), so roughly one Balance Druid in eight has already switched.

That lines up with the cast data: Sszorak is the only one of these fights with zero Starfall casts in the opening two minutes — it is pure single target, and the hero tree ranking flips accordingly.

These two are a choice pair inside Elune's Chosen — on every boss their two percentages sum to exactly the Elune's Chosen adoption figure, so every player running that hero tree picks one or the other.

It is the single most fight-dependent decision in the spec: an 89.7-point spread. Node 117177 runs 82.6–95.0% on the AoE-heavy fights, then collapses to 5.4% on Sszorak, where 117178 takes 82.3%. Nek'zali is the genuinely contested one at 35/59.

Reading these numbers

Do not compare DPS across bosses. The Lost Explorers shows 217.8k and Nek'zali 154.2k — that is the encounter, not the build. Only compare within a row.

Consensus varies a lot. Nek'zali's top build holds only 19.3% of parses with four class trees inside nine points of each other; Sszorak's top build holds 73.5%. A low number means the community has not settled, not that the listed build is wrong.
